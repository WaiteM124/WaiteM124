## Agent workflow

1. **Keep scope to the task.** Change only what the request requires. Do not
   expand into redesign, content rewrites, or unrelated repo hygiene.
2. **State assumptions in the PR.** Record what you assumed, what you verified,
   and what you deliberately left alone.
3. **Never weaken checks to make them pass.** If lint or link checks fail on

4. **Verify before claiming.** When stating that a link works or a metric is

5. **One concern per PR** unless the requester asks otherwise.
